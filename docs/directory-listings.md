## Long description (~200 words, for Capterra/G2 "About")

Roomward is the operations command center for hospitality properties. It pulls the work that
usually lives in group texts, paper logs, and spreadsheets into one place every team can see.

Maintenance teams log work orders in seconds, assign them to the right person, attach photos,
and track each one from open to resolved with a full history — nothing falls through the cracks
between shifts. Preventive maintenance schedules keep every asset (HVAC, pumps, elevators,
appliances) on a recurring plan, with reminders before something fails. Housekeeping runs a live
board — dirty, cleaning, ready — and the front desk sees which rooms are ready the moment they
are. A color-coded floor plan shows the whole property at a glance, and built-in team chat keeps
maintenance, housekeeping, and the front desk coordinated and in context.

Roles and permissions are fully configurable, so each role lands on exactly what matters to it.
Reporting shows what's breaking, how fast you close, and where time goes. And because Roomward
syncs two-way with RoomMaster and Eptura, it adds a live operations layer on top of your PMS
rather than asking you to rip anything out.

Per-property pricing, a 14-day free trial, and no credit card to start.

## Feature bullets (paste into a features field)

- Live, color-coded property floor plans
- Work orders: log, assign, prioritize, photo-document, and close
- Preventive maintenance scheduling on every asset, with due-date reminders
- Asset register (make, model, serial, location, condition, service history)
- Live housekeeping board (dirty → cleaning → ready)
- Front-desk request intake
- Team chat, organized by channel, encrypted in transit and at rest
- Configurable roles and permissions per team
- Reporting and analytics (open/close rates, by room, category, and tech)
- Two-way PMS sync: RoomMaster, Eptura (Opera, Cloudbeds, Maestro available)
- Works on phones, tablets, and desktop — nothing to install

## Who it's for

Independent and small-group hotels, resorts, lodges, and inns that want maintenance,
housekeeping, and the front desk working from one system — without ripping out their PMS.

## Differentiators (for "why choose" / pitch fields)

- One app for the **whole property**, not just a maintenance ticket queue — housekeeping,
  front desk, and maintenance actually talk to each other.
- A **live operations layer on top of your PMS**, synced two-way — adds to RoomMaster/Eptura
  instead of replacing them.
- Simple **per-property pricing** with everything included, not per-seat metering that
  punishes you for adding the housekeeper.
